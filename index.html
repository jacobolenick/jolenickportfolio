Senior Design Systems Designer · General Motors

I design the systems behind the screens in your car.

I’m a Senior Design Systems Designer at General Motors. I turn colors, type and spacing into tokens, tokens into components, and components into the products people actually touch.

I’ve designed for some of the most recognized companies in the world, on projects that have reached over a billion users and billions in revenue. I care about systems that stay clear as they grow.

01 What a design systems designer does

01Foundations

Color, type, spacing and elevation become variables and tokens — one source of truth with light and dark modes built in.

02Components

I build Figma libraries for scale: well-structured properties and variants so a small set of components covers a huge range of screens.

03Code

I connect Figma to Claude Code and Cursor through the Figma MCP server to code accurate components — an agentic design system that matches the library.

04Adoption

Docs, office hours and advocacy. Product teams ship consistent UI faster across phone, web, TV — and now the dashboard.
